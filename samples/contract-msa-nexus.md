WHEREAS, Client desires to retain Provider to perform software development and managed cloud infrastructure services, and Provider agrees to perform such services under the terms set forth herein.

## 1. SERVICES & SCOPE OF WORK

1.1 Services. Provider shall render technical services, software engineering, and managed cloud infrastructure architecture as detailed in mutually executed Statements of Work ("SOW") incorporated into this Agreement.

1.2 Amendments. Any modifications to the scope, timeline, or deliverables specified in an active SOW must be executed in writing via a formal Change Order signed by both parties.

## 2. COMPENSATION, INVOICING & PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 Fees. Client shall pay Provider the professional fees set forth in each applicable SOW. Initial monthly recurring retainer fees under SOW-01 are fixed at \$28,500.00 USD per month.

2.2 Payment Terms. All invoices issued by Provider shall be due and payable within Net 30 days from the date of invoice issuance.   
Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory rate allowed by law.

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

3.1 Work Product. Upon full payment of all applicable invoices, all custom software deliverables created specifically for Client under an SOW ("Deliverables") shall be deemed "work made for hire" and owned exclusively by Client.

3.2 Pre-Existing IP. Provider retains sole ownership of all pre-existing software, frameworks, code libraries, and proprietary AI utilities utilized in rendering the Services ("Provider IP"). Provider grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use Provider IP embedded in Deliverables.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION

4.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" includes all technical, business, financial, and operational data disclosed by one party to the other. Confidential Information shall be held in strict confidence for a period of three (3) years post-termination.

## 5. TERM, TERMINATION & LIABILITY LIMITS

5.1 Term & Renewal. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of twelve (12) months. It shall automatically renew for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to term expiration.

5.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure such breach within 30 calendar days of receiving written notice.

5.3 Limitation of Liability. Except for breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence, total aggregate liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client to Provider in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW & EXECUTION

6.1 Jurisdiction. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
